# Ledger: presenting estimates without losing their context

**Live project:** [BioInfo AI Studio Ledger](https://studio.jonathanrapisarda.dev/ledger)

## Problem

A catalogue of different products and services needs a consistent way to present estimates. Combining estimated labor, actual costs and proposed fees into a single figure would make it hard to tell what is known and what is assumed.

## Reporting design

The public Ledger displays an itemized catalogue and separates estimated replacement labor, context-weighted values, recorded cash cost and listed fee or proposal. Where costs have not been recorded, the interface identifies them as unknown. It also makes the published ledger available for download.

The design emphasizes readable labels, separate measures and disclosures so a reader can understand what each number represents before using it in a decision. The ledger can be inspected as a working public example rather than as a mockup.

## Evidence and limits

Values shown are estimates with disclosed limits. GOLD is the Studio's accounting unit; the public page explicitly distinguishes the figures from revenue and a wallet balance. Replacement labor is not a complete replacement cost when other costs are unknown. No client results or financial returns are implied.
